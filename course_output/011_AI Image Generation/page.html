BASIC IMAGE CREATION

Create an image of a humanoid lizard man. Make it cinematic, but keep the man looking friendly.

Change the outfit of the lizard man to a suit. Keep everything else the same.

BASIC IMAGE EDITING

Remove the phone and smartphone gimbal from the desk.

On the left hand side of the desk add a small cute kitten.

Colorize this image.

Open up the eyes of the girl who was blinking.

ENVIRONMENT CHANGES

Replace the gray clouds in the sky with a beautiful golden hour sunset.

Keep the girl and fish exactly the same, but change the image so she is waist deep in a pond surrounded by lily pads.

Change the office background to a futuristic YouTuber office designed for a techno music creator.

The room glows with neon blue and purple LED lights reflecting off sleek metallic walls. Dual ultra wide monitors display waveforms and mixing software with a digital audio workstation and MIDI controllers on a modern black desk.

Acoustic foam panels with geometric patterns line the walls, while vinyl records and neon signs add personality. A professional mic on a boom arm, studio headphones, and a camera setup with a softbox light are ready for streaming.

STYLE CHANGES

Change the style of the image to a childish cartoon, as if it was hand-drawn by a five year old.

Keep the man exactly the same, but change the style of everything else in the image to appear hand-drawn, as if it was drawn by a five year old kid.

Change the style of image one so it matches the cartoon animation style of image two.

Make sure to change the style of both the man and the background.

PROFESSIONAL HEADSHOTS

Create a professional headshot of the man from the picture.

Change the background of this image to a well-lit wall with the color #HEXCODE

INTERIOR + EXTERIOR DESIGN

Maintain the same exact bathroom layout, but redesign it with a much more modern style.

Improve the landscaping of this backyard and add a nice pool with a water slide.

Add a fire pit next to the pool.

TIME PERIOD REIMAGINING

Reimagine this couple, but place them in a classic 1920s photo shoot style scene.

(Replace 1920s with any decade like 1930s, 1940s, 1950s, 1980s, 1990s)

Place them in a future like The Jetsons cartoon.

COMBINING MULTIPLE IMAGES

Create a new image where the man from image one is giving the man from image two the silver plaque award that the man in image two is currently holding.

Create an entirely new image featuring the perfume bottle from image one placed in a beach environment similar to image two.

The camera angle for the new photo should be from ground level, somewhat of a tight shot focused on the perfume bottle, which is resting in the sand on the beach.

Create a new image featuring the perfume bottle from image one and the man from image two.

The man should be standing center frame in the image. In one hand he holds the perfume bottle to the camera. With his other hand, he points at the perfume bottle.

The perfume bottle should be in perfect focus, where the man in the background is slightly blurred and out of focus.

The man is looking at the camera and smiling.

Change the man's outfit from image one so it matches the outfit from image two.

YOUTUBE THUMBNAILS

Create a 16 by 9 YouTube thumbnail image for a camera review video.

It’s a close up shot of the man from image one holding the camera from image two up to the camera.

The focus of the image is clearly on the camera in the foreground, but slightly blurred.

In the back you can see the man's face. His expression is concerned.

CAMERA ANGLE CHANGES

Change the camera angle so it is a close up shot showing only the man's feet as he walks.

Change the camera angle from image one so that it is a new shot looking over the shoulder of the spider back at the man.

Use the face from image two as a reference for how the man should look.

CHARACTER CONSISTENCY

Using the lizard man from image one, create a new image showing the lizard man in hiking clothes at the top of a mountain in Switzerland.

Using the lizard man from image one, create a new image showing the lizard man in a high stakes board meeting.

He has placed his hands on the side of his head, indicating that he is very stressed out.

On a large whiteboard in the boardroom, there is a graph with text SALES at the top.

The graph is trending down, indicating that his business is struggling.

RECTANGLE PLACEMENT METHOD

Recreate the image.

Add a white carpet on the floor marked by the red rectangle.

Add a tall light in the area marked by the white rectangle.

Add a large plant in the area marked by the blue rectangle.

SKETCH TO FINAL IMAGE

Recreate the image and add a very large spaceship in the sky off in the distance, following along with the basic sketch provided.
