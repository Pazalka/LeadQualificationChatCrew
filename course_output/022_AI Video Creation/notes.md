Prompt Framework:
🔹 Image to Video Prompt Requirements
Subject Action
→ What is the subject doing?
Examples:
“The woman is turning slowly to face the camera.”
“A bird is soaring across a mountain ridge.”
“A samurai stands still as cherry blossoms fall.”
Camera Movement
→ How is the camera moving?
Examples:
“A slow dolly-in from behind.”
“360° orbit around the subject.”
“Cinematic handheld zoom out.”
Emotion / Mood / Tone
→ What should the viewer feel?
Examples:
“Evokes loneliness and wonder.”
“Feels tense and dramatic.”
“Hopeful and inspiring.”
🔹 Additional “text to video” prompt requirements
Subject(s)
Clearly define what or who is in the scene:
Is it a person, animal, object, or something fantastical?
Are there multiple subjects?
Does the focus shift from one subject to another?
Shot Type / Framing
→ How is the subject composed in the shot?
Examples:
“Close-up of the subject’s eyes.”
“Wide establishing shot of a mountain range.”
“Over-the-shoulder view of a man reading a letter.”
Environment / Setting
→ Where is the scene taking place?
Examples:
“Inside a cozy cabin during a snowstorm.”
“On a moving train at sunset.”
“Deep in a dense forest with fog.”
Visual Style
→ What style should the AI try to mimic?
Examples:
Hyper-realistic
Pixar-style 3D animation
Studio Ghibli-inspired
Marvel comics aesthetic
(Note: Not all models support every style equally well.)
🔹 Bonus Enhancements (Optional but Powerful)
Lighting Style
→ What kind of lighting is used in the scene?
Consider:
Color temperature: warm, cool, neutral
Light source: front-lit, backlit, side-lit, low lighting
Time of day: morning, noon, golden hour, night
Atmosphere / Environmental Effects
→ Add realism and texture to the scene
Examples:
Fog, rain, snow, wind, smoke, dust, embers
“Golden hour light with floating dust particles.”
“Rain trickling down a glass window in the background.”
Color Palette
→ Define the tone and feel through color
Examples:
“Muted vintage tones with a grainy film texture.”
“High-saturation neons like Blade Runner.”
“Warm, earth-toned medieval aesthetic.”

Bubbles drift past while rays of light pierce through the surface. The moment blends adrenaline and calm focus, framed as a dynamic follow shot that widens slightly underwater to reveal the diver against illuminated blue depths. Set during golden hour along a rugged coast, the scene shifts from warm sunlit tones to cool underwater hues in a hyper cinematic, high contrast visual style with soft depth of field and natural motion blur.
SAMURAI
A lone samurai in weathered armor and a masked rival in dark robes engage in a rapid, precise sword duel. Their blades clash intensely as the camera dynamically tracks and circles them with dramatic push-ins during impacts. The mood is tense, fierce, and focused, filled with honor and determination. Framed in a medium-wide shot, both fighters remain visible with brief close emphasis on blade contact and footwork. Set in a misty bamboo forest at dawn with drifting leaves and soft light beams. Rendered in a cinematic, high contrast style with subtle film grain inspired by classic samurai cinema and modern action films.

The camera performs a slow cinematic dolly-in from a medium-wide shot into a subtle over-the-shoulder perspective with slight handheld movement. The mood is calm, introspective, and warmly inspiring. Framing gradually tightens into a more intimate perspective while keeping soft environmental context.
