AI Clones
The process of creating AI video clones is TWO STEPS.
You always need to start by creating your
image clone
.
From there, we use a variety of different
AI tools to animate that "clone image" into a video
.
The specific AI tool you use will vary depending on the type of video you are creating.
If you are creating
short dynamic clips
(for example a video of your AI clone skydiving) you would use tools like Kling or Google's VEO3.1
If you were creating
longer form talking head clips
, you would use tools like Heygen or Hedra.
We have full tutorials showing exactly how to do these things in the "AI Video Creation" section of this course, which is coming up next!
I just wanted to include this written post in the course so you are aware the "clone process" is broken down into two parts, and we cover both parts in depth.
