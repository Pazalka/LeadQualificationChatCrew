Lesson Recap:
Train ChatGPT on your existing or new channel, by submitting the following info:
A brief description of your channel, what type of content do you create, what makes your content unique, who is your typical viewer, and what does that viewer like MOST about you and your content
A list of similar channels to yours, or channels that you aspire to be like
If you have posted videos already, give ChatGPT the best titles from your most successful videos
Prompt Template: "Can you help me come up with ten video ideas, each with their own unique, engaging, and curiosity driven title"
From there, pick your favorite title and create 5-10 variations of that title
Prompt Template: "I really like this idea [paste favorite title] can you please come up with 5 variations of this title, each one engaging, curiosity driven, and under 60 characters"
After that, either pick your favorite title and optimize for search or ask ChatGPT which it feels is best optimized for search (this is very important if you're a new channel)
Prompt Templates:
"Can you please optimize this title [paste title] for youtube SEARCH"
"Of the titles you just suggested, which do you think is most optimized for youtube SEARCH"
Then finally, it's time to start planning or writing. You have two options here.
Either write the outline yourself, and then paste that into CHAT and ask it to write your script
OR
Ask ChatGPT for a video outline to get the gears moving in your brain
