AI Ad Writing Prep Worksheet
Use this fill-in-the-blank template to organize your offer details and feed them into ChatGPT to generate high-converting, emotionally resonant ads.
1.
What is the name of your product or service?
Content Accelerator Bootcamp
1.
What transformation does your offer provide?
Go from overwhelmed new creator to confident content strategist in 30 days.
2.
Who is your ideal client?
Busy professionals in their 30s to 50s who want to launch a digital side business but feel overwhelmed by tech and content creation.
3.
What specific problems does your audience struggle with?
They feel stuck, burned out, and confused by conflicting advice online.
4.
What have they tried before that didn’t work?
They’ve watched free YouTube tutorials, bought generic templates, and tried to figure it out solo.
5.
What other options do they have (besides your offer)?
Hire a coach, keep trying random tips, or give up.
6.
What are the flaws or challenges with those other options?
Coaches are expensive, DIY takes too long, and burnout is real.
7.
What makes your offer different / better?
List the unique mechanisms, frameworks, or delivery methods that set you apart.
8.
What is included in your offer?
List features, bonuses, community access, templates, live calls, etc.
9.
List 1–4 social proof stories (testimonials or case studies)
Chris grew his YouTube channel from 0 to 20K in 3 months using this system.
10.
What is the price of your offer?
$197 one-time payment or 3 monthly payments of $75.
11.
What is the urgency of this offer?
Limited-time bonus expires this Friday at midnight. Plus, the longer they wait, the more overwhelmed they stay.
12.
Do you offer any guarantees?
14-day money-back guarantee if you’re not satisfied with the results.
13.
What call-to-action do you want the ad to end with?
Click here to enroll instantly and start your transformation today.
Once filled out, you can use a prompt like:
"Act as a professional ad copywriter. Based on the details below, write a high-converting Facebook ad in a casual tone using the hook, problem, solution, offer details, call to action framework. Make it emotionally engaging and under 150 words."
Then paste in your completed worksheet.
